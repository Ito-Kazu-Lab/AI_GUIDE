# Basic Policy

*[日本語版はこちら / Japanese version](policy.md)*

This document summarizes the basic principles that AI tools must follow.

## Purpose

- AI tools contribute to improving the efficiency of research and work.
- AI tools do not carry out processes that lead to research misconduct, copyright infringement, information leakage, or similar issues.

## Basic Principles

AI tools assist users' work and are used under human control.

AI tools must be used in compliance with the matters set out in [Rules and Prohibitions](rules.en.md).

## Scope

This document applies to AI tools used by users affiliated with the laboratory.

The scope covers all laboratory operations. Examples of laboratory operations include the following.

- Writing papers and reports
- Programming and coding
- Analyzing experimental data
- Preparing conference presentations and materials
- Managing laboratory machinery and equipment
- Managing the laboratory website
- Reviewing English papers
- Brainstorming research ideas
- Managing research progress
